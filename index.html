Engagement process

How our US accounting engagements work

A simple, repeatable cycle so your books are closed and reviewed every month.

  1. 1

    Discovery and Scoping

    We understand your entities, transaction volumes, systems and reporting needs, then agree a clear scope, timeline and fee.

  2. 2

    Secure Onboarding

    We work inside your own accounting platform and tools with role-based access, and document your processes in a short onboarding checklist.

  3. 3

    Monthly Processing Cycle

    Transactions are recorded on an agreed schedule, accounts are reconciled, and open questions are sent to you as one consolidated list.

  4. 4

    Close, Report and Review

    You receive a monthly close package and review call; at year end, tax-ready files go straight to your CPA.

  • Dedicated AccountantA full-time resource working only on your books, in your systems, as part of your team.
  • Fixed Monthly PackageScoped by entities and transaction volume, and billed at a fixed monthly fee.
  • CPA Firm OutsourcingWhite-label staff support for US CPA firms, year-round or for busy season.
  • Confidentiality and Data SecurityEngagement-level NDAs, role-based access and work carried out only within client-approved systems.
  • India–US Time-Zone AdvantageWork shared at the end of your US day is processed during India hours, so updated books are waiting the next morning.

For businesses in India

GST Compliance and Advisory

Complete GST support for Indian businesses, from registration and periodic returns to refunds, audits and representation before GST authorities up to the GST Appellate Tribunal (GSTAT).

Registration and Periodic Compliance

  • GST registration and amendments
  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filing
  • ITC reconciliation with GSTR-2B
  • E-invoicing and e-way bill support
  • Compliance calendar and due-date tracking

Annual Compliance and Audit

  • Annual return (GSTR-9) and reconciliation statement (GSTR-9C)
  • GST audit
  • Certification under indirect tax laws
  • GST health checks of processes and credits

Exports, Refunds and LUT

  • Letter of Undertaking (LUT) for zero-rated exports
  • GST refund applications and follow-up
  • Export of services and place-of-supply advisory
  • Refund-related certificates

Advisory, Notices and Litigation

  • Transaction and classification advisory
  • Drafting replies to GST notices
  • GST assessments
  • Advance ruling applications
  • Representation before GST authorities up to GSTAT

Exporting services to overseas clients? We help Indian service exporters treat supplies correctly as zero-rated, file LUTs on time and claim refunds of accumulated input tax credit, so working capital is not locked up with the tax department.
